Step 0

Get access

One tap — no forms, nothing to copy. The bot handles it.

1
Open the bot & tap Start Open @malakut_expense_report_bot and tap Start (or just send any message).
2
Your request goes to the admin — automatically The bot already knows who you are, so it forwards your access request to the administrator on its own. No Telegram ID to find, copy or send.
3
You're in the moment they approve You'll get a 🎉 Access granted message right here in the chat. Then type /start to begin.
💡 Nothing needed on your side until then — just wait for the approval message. It's usually quick.
Step 1

Open a pack

A "pack" = one expense report for one trip or period.

Pack → Report. A pack is what you're putting together — receipts, documents, per-diem. When you submit it, the pack becomes a report and accounting takes over: they approve an amount or send it back. One pack open at a time.
You: /newpack Zurich May 2026
Bot: Pack opened.
Pack #68: «Zurich May 2026» [open]
  Created:  2026-05-10T09:12
  Currency: AED
Now send photos of receipts or PDF documents — I'll add them to this pack.
Bot: 📸 Send your first receipt — photo or document — to add it to this pack.
Each photo or file is added separately. Several pages of one document? Use the button «📚 Multi-page document».

Typed several expenses in one message — “a taxi 50 AED and a hotel 300 AED”? Each one becomes its own line, with its own read-out and its own ✏️ Fix · 🗑 Remove. The same expense named twice — same vendor, amount and currency — is added once.

Under that second message: 📚 Multi-page document, 💬 Comment · 🧑 On behalf, ✏️ Rename pack · 🗑 Delete pack. Once receipts are in, the message after each one carries this bar:

📚 Multi-page document
👁 Preview PDF
📋 Items so far
📤 Submit for review
💬 Comment
🧑 On behalf
🗑 Delete pack
/showpack shows a slightly different set: no 📋 Items so far (that is what you are looking at), and ✏️ Rename pack next to 🗑 Delete pack.

One pack open at a time. Ask for a new one while another is open and the bot names the one in the way, offering to carry on with it — or to submit it and start fresh. Name it after a city and month, a project, anything that identifies the period.
Step 2

Add receipts

Photo or file — a receipt is often a PDF (tickets, invoices). The bot reads either.

You: (sends a photo of the receipt)
Bot: ✅ Added:
Vendor: Marriott Hotel Dubai
Amount: 520.00 AED
Type:   Hotel
Date:   2026-05-11
✏️ Fix
🗑 Remove

No confirmation needed. A single receipt — photo or file — is read and saved straight away, and you see every field it recognised — so a wrong one is obvious immediately. Keep sending photos; each becomes its own line. Two exceptions wait for you: a business meeting with nobody named (its card asks who was there — slide “Expense categories”) and a document that looks like a line already in the pack (next slide). While one of them waits on a photo or file you sent, the next photo or file is turned away — “⚠️ One at a time — decide on the receipt above first, then send this photo again.” (“…this document again.” for a file), with 🚫 Cancel that one, which cancels the card above, not the new file. Answer the card, then send the rest again.

An expense you only describe in words is saved straight away too. If the bot took it for one with a document behind it and no file was there, it says so under the read-out: “ℹ️ No document is attached to this line — send it if you have one.”

🧾 Send the real document, not just the payment slip. For a hotel that's the invoice or folio (guest name, check-in and check-out); for a flight the ticket or boarding pass (route and date). A bank confirmation proves money moved but not who stayed or flew when — the bot points this out before you submit, and you can still send it as it is.
⚠️ If a save ever fails, the bot says so — which line, and why. “⚠️ That receipt wasn't saved, and nothing half-saved was left behind. What: Hotel Adria · 1136.00 EUR. Why: writing the file did not work.” The reason is one of report #42 is submitted, writing the file did not work, something went wrong on my side while saving it, or the first line of the failed step's own answer. What to do next depends on it:
• the report is already with accounting: “Report #42 is submitted — it can't take new lines. Reopen it from /myreports, or start a new report with /newpack, then add this expense again.”
• you had sent a photo or PDF: “Send the photo or PDF again and I'll read it from scratch. If the second try fails the same way, /feedback puts it in front of a person instead of another retry.”
• you had described it in words: “Tell me about it again and I'll add it. If the second try fails the same way, /feedback puts it in front of a person instead of another retry.”
If a multi-page document was being collected, the next step is followed by “📚 Your 3 page(s) are still here — this did not touch them. Send the next page, or tap Document complete.”, with ✅ Document complete · 🚫 Cancel on top.
Under it are the pack buttons and 💬 Leave feedback — the second step is one tap, not a command to remember.

A card still on screen when its report went to accounting is never saved: its ✅ Confirm answers Not saved with the message above, and if you don't tap it, the bot's next answer says it once, the same way.

When you asked the bot to attach a document to a line, or to file a Travel Request, and writing that file fails, the bot answers in its own words — and under that answer, always, comes “Still holding your PDF.” — “…your photo.” for a photo — with 🚫 Discard it. The file is kept: tell the bot again what it is, or discard it. What you never get is a silent card with the receipt missing from the report.
Step 2 · a repeat

Already in the pack?

Sent the same invoice twice, or one more page of a document you already added? The bot doesn't count it twice — and doesn't turn it away. It asks.

When a photo or PDF you send reads as a line already saved in this pack — the same amount, the same currency, and a vendor that looks the same (shares a word) — nothing is saved yet:

Bot: Looks like line 2 — Anthropic · 10.67 USD · 2026-10-03 — is already in the pack. This document:
📎 Add it as another page of line 2
➕ Separate expense — add as a new line
🚫 Discard
📎
Another page of that line The document goes onto line 2 as one more document. No line is added, and the amount is counted once: “📎 Filed as another page of line 2 — Anthropic · 10.67 USD. The amount stays once.” If line 2 had no document yet, this one becomes its document: “📎 Filed as the document of line 2 — …”
➕
A separate expense Two identical taxi rides happen. “➕ Adding Anthropic · 10.67 USD as a separate expense.” — and it lands like any receipt: ✅ Added, with ✏️ Fix · 🗑 Remove. A multi-page document gets its card, waiting for ✅ Confirm.
🚫
Discard The file is deleted and nothing is added: “🚫 Discarded … — send the next PDF whenever you're ready.”
📋 Several lines look the same? The question lists them — “Looks like these lines are already in the pack:”, one line each — with a 📎 Add it as another page of line … button for each, up to three. The line numbers are the ones /showpack shows.

👆 A tap answers it. Typing yes, ok or no saves nothing and drops nothing — the question stays, with its buttons. Until it is answered, a submit stops at it (slide “Submitting with a file still on hold”).

📄 Only a document is asked about. An expense you describe in words is saved as before; a supporting document is never asked about — it adds nothing to the total; and a line in another pack is not a repeat. A multi-page document that repeats a line is asked about when you tap ✅ Document complete, before its card.
Step 2 · continued

Several pages, one document

A hotel folio or a garage invoice often runs over pages — and the total is on the last one.

Three ways in. Whichever you use, the pages become one receipt:

📚
Tap “Multi-page document” Then send the pages one by one and tap ✅ Document complete.
🤖
Let the bot notice Send page 1; when the bot sees it continues, it says so and asks “How should I treat it?” — 📚 Multi-page — I'll send the rest, ✅ Just this page or 🚫 Discard it.
📷
Just send the next page If page 1 was a photo, answering that question with the next photo works too — the pages join up by themselves. A PDF does not join this way: send the whole document as one PDF.

When it's read, check the total before it's stored:

✅ Confirm
✏️ Change
🚫 Cancel
➕ Add another page
This card waits for ✅ Confirm before saving (the only other card that waits is a business meeting's, for the names): the total is assembled across pages, so it shows what it read — including the page count — and waits. Remembered a page you missed? Add another page keeps everything already photographed. Already have a PDF? Just send the file.

It waits for your tap, and nothing else saves it. Write about something else in the meantime and the card stays as it is; under the bot's answer one line reminds you: “⏳ The card for Hotel Adria · 1136.00 EUR is still waiting — tap ✅ Confirm or 🚫 Cancel on it.”

✏️ Change fixes Type, Vendor, Amount, Currency, Date or Note before you confirm. A new currency is shown on the card with its AED value at today's rate — “Amount: 367.25 USD = 1348.70 AED”; if the rate can't be fetched, nothing changes: “I couldn't get today's exchange rate, so nothing was changed. Try again in a minute.” Corrected it in words instead (“the date is 30 September”)? The same card comes back updated — “✏️ Updated — check it again.” — still waiting for Confirm.

A document can make two cards — a hotel folio and a separate restaurant bill on it, say. Each card's buttons act on that card only, but the document goes to one line: confirm first the card whose document it is. Confirm the restaurant first and the bot asks “📎 I'm still holding the PDF I read as Hotel Adria · 1136.00 EUR. Is it the document for Adria Restaurant · 85.00 EUR?” — Yes gives the document to the restaurant and withdraws the hotel card together with its expense: “🚫 The card for Hotel Adria · 1136.00 EUR is withdrawn — its document is now on Adria Restaurant · 85.00 EUR.” The 1136 EUR is not saved, and that card's Confirm then answers “This draft is no longer active.” No saves the restaurant without a document, and the hotel keeps the PDF when you confirm it. Typed yes with two cards waiting? The bot asks “2 cards are waiting — which one should I add?” with a ✅ Confirm … button per card; typed no — “2 cards are waiting — which one should I cancel?” with a 🚫 Cancel … button per card. Corrected the total in words and the bot made a new line from it? It asks whether the document belongs to the new line; answer Yes and the old card is withdrawn: “🚫 The card for Hotel Adria · 130.98 EUR is withdrawn — its document is now on Hotel Adria · 350.00 EUR.”

The bot never deletes, on its own, a document you sent that is not saved yet. If it drops a card it made — it can, when it misreads what you said — or you tell it “actually, never mind” about a photo it was asking about, the document stays with the bot and is named under its answer: “📎 Still holding the photo you sent — tell me what it is and I'll file it.” with 🚫 Discard it. That button, 🚫 Cancel on a card, your own typed no to a card, or /cancel are what delete such a document — and the slide “What happens to a file the bot is holding” lists where one can still go: the next upload replacing it, and /cancel while pages are being collected. A line that is already saved is different: 🗑 Remove, or asking in words — delete #2, “delete this pack” — removes the line or the whole open pack, with their documents, and asked in words, the bot can do it without a button to confirm.

(A file sent while a document is being collected is held, as below.)

Step 2 · a file on hold

“Is it the document for this line?”

Sometimes the bot keeps a file back — a save failed and it kept the copy, or it asked you something about it first. It never quietly pins that file onto a different line.

Say it is holding a hotel invoice, and you now describe a taxi ride in words. The file was not read for the taxi, so the bot asks:

Bot: 📎 I'm still holding the PDF I read as Motel One Zurich · 1136.00 CHF.
Is it the document for Careem · 50.00 AED?
📎 Yes — attach it to this line
➕ No — different expense, save without it

For a proof document the second button is ➕ No — a different document, and then nothing is filed: “Nothing filed for … — send that document itself and I'll file it.”

If more than one line wants the file and it matches none of them:

Bot: 📎 I'm still holding the PDF I read as Motel One Zurich · 1136.00 CHF.
It was asked for by more than one line, and it matches none of them. Which one is it the document for?
📎 Attach it to Careem · 50.00 AED
📎 Attach it to Talabat · 85.00 AED
✋ None of these — keep it

After your tap the question is rewritten to say what happened: → attached to Careem · 50.00 AED or → kept. If the save after Yes was refused or failed, it reads → not attached, the next message says why — for a failed write, “⚠ That one wasn't saved — writing the file did not work. I still have what you sent, so nothing was lost. Send it again, or tell me what it is and I'll try with the copy I'm holding.” — and the same question is shown again. The line and the file are both kept: Yes tries again, No saves the line without the file. Answer No in the first place and the line is saved without the document; the file stays with the bot, named, with a 🚫 Discard it button.

🕓 An attach button never sends the file to a different line. Yes and Attach it to … each name one line. A button on an older copy of the question either attaches the file to exactly the line it names, or leaves the file where it is — never to another line. Tap the newest copy to be sure what you are answering. When an older button is turned down, its message changes to — this question was answered or replaced — and the current question is shown again. If the line that button named can no longer take a file, it reads — not attached: that line can no longer take a file —; and when nothing is left to ask about, the bot says “Not attached — that line can no longer take a file.” followed by “📎 Still holding … — tell me what it is and I'll file it.” with 🚫 Discard it.
📷 A photo held from earlier is asked about too. If the bot answered your photo with a question — tell me what it is — and you reply by describing the expense, it does not attach the photo on its own; it asks, and one tap settles it:
“📎 I'm still holding the photo you sent. Is it the document for Careem · 50.00 AED?” — 📎 Yes — attach it to this line / ➕ No — different expense, save without it.
If several lines want it: “It was asked for by more than one line. Which one is it the document for?” When the bot reads the expense straight from the photo you have just sent, nothing is asked. The extra tap is the price of never pinning a file to the wrong line; it may be removed later, but for now it is there.
👆 Only a tap answers this question. Typing yes or no while it is on screen attaches nothing and settles nothing — the bot reads it as an ordinary message. The one exception is a value the bot itself asked you for: tap ✏️ Change → Amount and type the number, and the number is taken.
🔗 The same goes when you ask in words — “attach it to my Careem line” — and the held file was read as something else: “That doesn't match Careem · 50.00 AED. Use it for Careem · 50.00 AED anyway?” with 📎 Yes — use it there and ✋ No — keep it for now.
Step 2 · a file on hold

What happens to a file the bot is holding

Where it stops and asks you — and, for now, where the file can still go without a word.

These stop, name the file, and let you decide:

📚
Tap “Multi-page document” “📎 I'm still holding the PDF I read as Motel One Zurich · 1136.00 CHF. A multi-page document would replace it. Tell me what it is so I can file it first — or discard it and start the document.” Buttons: 🗑 Discard it and start, and — only when the held file is a photo the bot has not read yet — 📚 Use it as page 1.
📄
“📚 Multi-page — I'll send the rest” on a PDF If the bot took a PDF for page 1 of a longer document, this button refuses and says why: “📎 I'm still holding the PDF … — it's a PDF, so it can't be page 1, and a multi-page document would replace it.” with 🗑 Discard it and start. The PDF stays; send the whole document as one PDF instead.
✅
Tap “Document complete” “📎 I'm still holding the PDF I read as Motel One Zurich · 1136.00 CHF. Your 3 page(s) are kept either way. What should I do with it?” Three buttons:
• 📄 File it as its own expense, then finish — a file the bot has already read becomes its own line, “✅ Added: Motel One Zurich · 1136.00 CHF (PDF attached).”, and your pages are assembled straight after, no second tap. A file not read yet is read first — “📄 Reading the photo you sent as its own expense…” — the pages stay, and then “📚 Your 3 page(s) are still here — tap Document complete when ready.” If filing fails: “⚠️ Couldn't file the PDF — {reason}. Still holding it; your 3 page(s) are kept.” and the same three buttons (“photo” for a photo).
• 🗑 Discard it, keep my pages — “🗑 Discarded the PDF I read as Motel One Zurich · 1136.00 CHF. Your 3 page(s) are still here.” with ✅ Document complete · 🚫 Cancel.
• ✅ Document complete — the same screen again until the file is settled.
📂
Switch to another pack 📂 Continue in /myreports moves you, and the file comes along: “📎 Still holding … — tell me what it is and I'll file it here, or discard it.” with 🚫 Discard it.
🔁
It looks like a line already in the pack The file waits with the question — 📎 Add it as another page of line 2, ➕ Separate expense — add as a new line or 🚫 Discard (slide “Already in the pack?”).
🚫
Type /cancel With a card on screen, it drops the card, not the held file: “📎 Still holding … — tell me what it is and I'll file it, or tap Discard.” with 🚫 Discard the held file. With nothing else pending, /cancel is the discard — and names what went: “🚫 Dropped the PDF I read as Motel One Zurich · 1136.00 CHF. Nothing was saved.” Whatever it dropped is said in one message; “🚫 Nothing was pending — you're clear either way.” only when there was nothing to drop.
🔑 Discard buttons are tied to what they discard. 🚫 Discard it, 🚫 Discard the held file, 🚫 Cancel on a receipt card, 🗑 Discard it and start and 🗑 Discard it, keep my pages each carry their own file or card. Tapped on an older message after that file or card has changed, the button touches nothing and says so in a pop-up — — that card is no longer active —, — that file is no longer held — or — nothing is held —. Sometimes the bot also puts what is current at the bottom of the chat; tap there. The buttons of a document being collected are tied the same way: — that document is no longer being collected —.
⚠️ Where a held file can still disappear.
• Sending the next photo or file while one file is held, with no card or document waiting, replaces the held one: “↻ Replaced your previous photo with this … — I only hold one at a time.” It does not say which file went, and calls it a photo even when it was a PDF. (A finished multi-page document is never replaced this way — see the slide on documents being collected.)
• /cancel while pages are being collected throws those pages away — it says so: “📚 Dropped the 3 page(s) you had started — they were never added.”
Submitting or deleting the pack no longer lets a held file go quietly — see the slide on submitting.
So: when the bot says it is still holding a document you need, tell it what the document is before you send the next one.

Just type the names and company under the card — Sam Wilson, ABC Corp — and the line is saved with them in its note, “Note: business lunch — with Sam Wilson, ABC Corp”, shown as ✅ Added with ✏️ Fix · 🗑 Remove. ✍️ Names & company does the same: the card says “Type the names and company.”, and you type them. ➕ Add without names — or typing add or skip — saves it as it was read; 🚫 Cancel or no drops it. Named them when you sent it — “add business lunch with Sam Wilson, ABC Corp — 300 AED”? Then the bot doesn't ask: the line is saved straight away, with the names.

💬 Whatever you type next is taken as the names — with these exceptions. An amount is not names: a number on its own (“500”) or an amount with its currency (“actually it was 500 AED”, “€480”) goes to the bot as an ordinary message, and the card keeps waiting. A number inside names, with no currency beside it (“Room 101 team, ABC Corp”), is part of the names. A command is a command. With two meeting cards waiting, typed names go onto neither: “2 cards are waiting for names — which one are these for? Tap it, then type the names again.”, with a ✍️ Names for Zuma · 300.00 AED button for each card. While a card waits, the bot's answers to your ordinary messages end with a line saying which card is waiting and for what — for one meeting card, “⏳ The card for Zuma · 300.00 AED is still waiting — type who you met, or tap ➕ Add without names or 🚫 Cancel on it.” After a command the bot knows (like /showpack) there is no such line; a mistyped one (/sumbit) is read as an ordinary message, and the line comes. Names wrong later? ✏️ Fix → Note (Step 4).
You can always override a category later — see Step 4 (Fix mistakes).

Currency takes a code (USD, eur), a sign (€, $, £, Ð) or a word (dirhams, UAE dirhams, euros). A word it does not know gets I don't know “…” as a currency. 🔢 How a typed amount is read — in Amount here, in ✏️ Change → Amount on a multi-page card, and in the accountant's /approve and ✏️ Other amount:
• 6117,37 is 6117.37 — a comma before one or two digits is the decimal point;
• 6,117.37, 6.117,37 and 6 117,37 are all 6117.37 — with both marks, the last one is the decimal point;
• 1,200 or 1.200 could be either (some currencies use three decimals), so the bot asks instead of guessing: “1,200” could be 1200 or 1.2 — I didn't guess. Send it again as 1200 or 1.2.
• not a number at all: That doesn't look like a number. Send the amount again, e.g. 1250.50. (/approve answers differently: I couldn't read the amount “…”. and shows how to write the command.)
• a different currency next to the number is not converted, and nothing is changed. On an AED line, Amount → 150 USD: “150 USD” is in USD, but this line is in AED. I didn't convert it, and nothing was changed. Send the amount in AED. If AED itself is wrong, tap ← Leave it above, then Currency. The line's own currency is fine — 150 AED, 150 dirhams. The currency is checked first, so 1,200 eur is refused for the currency, not asked about. On a multi-page card the words are “… but this receipt is in …”; for the accountant “… but the approved amount is in AED …” — after Report #54 was not approved. on /approve, and followed by Or 'cancel'. on Other amount.

Single receipts save on their own — except a business meeting with nobody named, which asks who was there, and a document that looks like a line already in the pack, which asks what it is. All dates are shown in YYYY-MM-DD format.
