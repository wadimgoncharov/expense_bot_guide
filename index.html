Step 0

Get access

One tap — no forms, nothing to copy. The bot handles it.

1
Open the bot & tap Start Open @malakut_expense_report_bot and tap Start (or just send any message).
2
Your request goes to the admin — automatically The bot already knows who you are, so it forwards your access request to the administrator on its own. No Telegram ID to find, copy or send.
3
You're in the moment they approve You'll get a 🎉 Access granted message right here in the chat. Then type /start to begin.
💡 Nothing needed on your side until then — just wait for the approval message. It's usually quick.
Step 1

Open a pack

A "pack" = one expense report for one trip or period.

Pack → Report. A pack is what you're putting together — receipts, documents, per-diem. When you submit it, the pack becomes a report and accounting takes over: they approve an amount or send it back. One pack open at a time.

Type in the Telegram chat:

You: /newpack Zurich May 2026
Bot: ✅ Pack #68 «Zurich May 2026» opened
📌
Naming tip City + month, project name, or any label that identifies the period.
🔒
One active pack at a time Stays open until /compile — that's when it becomes a report.

You: (sends a photo of the receipt)
No confirmation needed — just keep sending photos
Bot: ✅ Added — Marriott, 520.00 AED, Hotel, 2026-05-11
Wrong? ✏️ Fix any field · 🗑 Remove
📚 A document with several pages? Tap Multi-page document, send the pages one by one, then Document complete — they become one receipt and the bot reads the final total across all of them. Already a PDF? Just send it.

🧾 Attach the real document, not just the payment slip. For a hotel send the invoice/folio (guest + check-in/out dates); for a flight send the ticket or boarding pass (route + date). A bank slip alone doesn't show who stayed or flew when — the bot flags it before you submit.

Auto-detection

Expense categories

The bot reads each document and assigns a category automatically.

🏨
Hotel
Hotel, Airbnb, any accommodation
🍽
Business meetings
Restaurant, café, conference, client lunch
📦
Other
Everything that doesn't fit the above categories
🍽 For Business meetings (lunch, dinner, coffee), the bot asks who you met with so the report shows attendees — e.g. "Sam Wilson, ABC Corp — Q3 pipeline". Reply with names, or just say add to save without.
You can always override a category later — see Step 4 (Fix mistakes).

Step 4

Fix mistakes

Easiest way: tap ✏️ Change on the draft before adding. Already added? Run /showpack for item numbers, then:

change type for #3 to Hotel Reclassify an item's expense category
fix vendor for #1 — Marriott Correct a misread vendor name
fix amount for #2 — 150 Update a wrong amount
fix date for #1 — 2026-05-10 Correct a misread date
attach it to receipt #3 Scan didn't save? If /preview shows "Scan not saved", re-send the photo/PDF, then say this
delete #2 Remove an item from the pack
⚠️ The bot always asks for confirmation before applying any change. You must reply yes to proceed.
Business trip

Per-diem allowance

For business trips, send your WebHR Travel Request as a photo or PDF — the bot computes per-diem automatically.

1
Send the Travel Request — photo or PDF The WebHR form with Travel Start Date and Travel End Date. A clear photo of the form works just as well as a PDF.
2
The bot extracts dates and computes the allowance Rate: 300 AED for each of the first 2 days · 200 AED for each remaining day.
💰
Per-diem appears on the cover of the final report Added to your total — and the original Travel Request is attached as a supporting document for accounting.
💡 A pack with only the Travel Request and no other receipts can still be submitted — the per-diem on its own is a valid reimbursement.
Step 5

Generate the report

When all receipts are confirmed, produce your PDF expense report.

👁
/preview
See the PDF without submitting. The pack stays yours.
✅
/compile
Submit it — the pack becomes a report and goes to accounting.
📤
You get the PDF in chat The report is submitted for accountant review — see the next slide for what happens then.
✅ Completeness check. On /compile the bot flags any line backed only by a payment slip (missing hotel invoice / flight ticket). Add the missing file and /compile again — or reply submit anyway to send it as-is.

For accountants

Reviewing reports

If you're an accountant, each submitted report arrives here as a PDF. Process it with these commands.

/pendingList all reports awaiting review
/report 54See report #54 — status + PDF
/approve 54Approve at the full submitted total
/approve 54 1200Approve a custom AED amount (e.g. after cutting items)
/return 54 …Send back for fixes with a note
/reply 54 …Message the employee
⚠ Gap flags. /pending and /report mark lines backed only by a payment slip (e.g. a hotel charge with no invoice, a flight with no ticket) — so you can ask for the missing document before approving.
💡 The number is the report id from /pending. #54, 54 or report 54 all work. The employee is notified automatically on approve/return. Type /review in the bot for this list anytime.
